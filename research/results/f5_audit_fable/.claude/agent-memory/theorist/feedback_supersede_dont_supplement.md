When you close a gap in someone else's proof, state in the document — in a dedicated section — that
the old ingredient is **superseded, not supplemented**, name the exact text to delete, and supply
splice text for the source document. Do not edit the other document yourself; that is the First
Mate's ledger job.

**Why:** the mission's parsimony requirement (V6, retained after the 2026-08-19 amendment) counts
statements. A new lemma that merely sits beside the old one grows the corpus and fails the mission
even if correct. A reader left with two versions of "Lemma E" cannot tell which is load-bearing.

**How to apply:** include a "Splice text" section quoting the replacement paragraph verbatim, plus
which status-table rows and open-item bullets to change. Also prefer a proof that *absorbs* nearby
machinery: replacing Lemma A's complex-analytic proof with a real-variable one kept the whole of
Theorem 2 inside real analysis at zero cost in statement count. See
[[feedback-document-before-numerics]].
